The Random Walk

A running notebook on the 1D random walk.

This site collects the theory, simulations, and interactive figures behind an ongoing project studying random walks through the lens of statistical mechanics and information theory — originating from a thermal physics course assignment and a continuation from there. The code behind these figures lives in the 1D-Random-Walk-Simulation repository.

1D Random Walk
1-dimensional random walk theory: formalizing notation and establishing equiprobable directions.
Boundary Conditions
Imposed reflecting boundaries: discussing symmetry of boundary conditions, step size smaller than 1, & scaling for time.
